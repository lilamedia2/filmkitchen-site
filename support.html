Support

Questions, bugs, or a camera we don't recognize yet

Start with the FAQ below. If it doesn't cover what you're seeing, email us directly and we'll help you sort it out.

Tested so far with the Fujifilm X-S20. Other X and GFX bodies are untested.

Which cameras does FujiKitchen support?

FujiKitchen is built to work with any Fujifilm X or GFX series camera that supports USB-C tethered shooting. That said, we've only tested it against the Fujifilm X-S20 so far, so behavior on other bodies isn't verified yet. If your camera doesn't appear when connected, check the connection question below before reaching out.

Does applying a recipe change my original RAW file?

No. Recipes are applied to a processed copy generated by your camera. Your original RAW file stays exactly as it was shot.

Can I build my own recipes, or only use saved ones?

Both. Build a recipe from the same parameters your camera exposes, film simulation, dynamic range, grain effect, white balance shift, highlight and shadow tone, color chrome effect, and sharpness, then save it to your library alongside anything you've saved from elsewhere.

My camera isn't detected over USB-C. What should I check?

Confirm the camera's USB connection mode is set for data, not mass storage or charge-only, and that the cable itself supports data transfer. Power the camera on before connecting rather than after. If it's still not detected, restarting both the app and the camera resolves most cases.

Can I import RAW files that aren't currently on my camera?

Yes. Use the Files import option to bring in RAW files you've already offloaded to your device, then apply recipes to them the same way.

How many photos should I import at once?

Fewer photos on the card means faster preview loading. At this time, we don't recommend importing more than 10 photos in a single batch; larger imports can slow preview generation noticeably. This isn't a hard limit enforced by the app, just current guidance while we improve performance on larger card libraries.

Does FujiKitchen upload or store my photos anywhere?

No. Your files stay on your device and your camera. Nothing is uploaded to an external server.

Still stuck?

Email us and include what you were trying to do. That's usually enough to reproduce the issue on our end.
