How it works

01

Connect over USB-C

Plug your Fujifilm camera into your phone. FilmKitchen talks to it directly, no desktop step in between.

02

Pick or build a recipe

Browse and compare recipes, or set your own dynamic range, grain, white balance shift, and tone parameters.

03

Apply, export, save

The recipe is processed by your camera, not your phone. Export the result and save it directly to your Photos App.

What's in the app

Compare recipes

See how tone curve, grain, white balance shift, and dynamic range change a look before you commit to it.

Build a library

Save recipes you find, or build your own from the same parameters your camera already exposes.

Camera-native processing

Recipes are applied by your camera's own image processor, so results match what you'd get shooting the recipe in-camera. Currently tested with the Fujifilm X-S20 only; other bodies haven't been verified yet.

Import from anywhere

Pull RAW files straight off a connected camera, or bring in files already offloaded to your Files app.
